Request account deletion

App: Momentum (com.fitness.momentum)

You can request that we delete your Momentum account and associated personal data. Use the steps below. Requests are handled by email.

Send an email to:
dev.ai.login@gmail.com

Subject line: Delete my Momentum account

Include:

  • The email address on your Momentum account
  • Confirmation that you want the account and associated data deleted
Email deletion request

What we delete

  • Your account / login
  • Profile and onboarding answers
  • Workout plans, logs, and week reviews tied to your account

Timing

We aim to complete deletion within 30 days of verifying your request. Some limited records may be retained longer only if required for security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

After deletion

You will no longer be able to sign in. Starting again requires creating a new account.
