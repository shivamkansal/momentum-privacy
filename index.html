Privacy Policy

App: Momentum (com.fitness.momentum)
Last updated: 8 August 2026

Momentum (“we”, “us”) is a personal fitness training app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using Momentum you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account data: email address and authentication credentials (password or Google sign-in).
  • Profile & onboarding answers: sex, age, height, weight, fitness goal, experience level, training schedule, equipment, injury/condition preferences, exercise likes/avoids, cardio preference, and optional free-text notes.
  • Workout data: plans we generate for you, sets logged (weights, reps, band levels, durations), week reviews, and related progress stats.
  • Device / app data: basic technical information needed to run the app and keep you signed in (for example session tokens stored on your device).

How we use information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Generate and update workout plans
  • Save workout logs and week reviews
  • Improve reliability and support (for example diagnosing account or sync issues)

We do not sell your personal information.

Where data is stored

Account and app data are stored using our cloud backend provider (Supabase). Data in transit is protected with HTTPS.

Third parties

  • Supabase: authentication and database hosting.
  • Google: only if you choose “Continue with Google” for sign-in.

We do not use third-party advertising SDKs in the app.

Health & fitness notice

Momentum provides general fitness guidance and workout planning. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. If you have a medical condition, injury, or are pregnant, consult a qualified clinician before starting a new training program.

Data retention

We keep your account and workout data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymize associated personal data from our systems within a reasonable period, except where we must retain limited records for legal or security reasons.

Your choices

  • You can edit profile and training preferences in the app.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data here: Request account deletion (or email dev.ai.login@gmail.com with subject “Delete my Momentum account” from the email on your account).

Children

Momentum is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
